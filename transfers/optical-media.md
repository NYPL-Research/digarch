# Optical Media  
There are 3 workflows for transferring optical media.  
The most common is Mass Optical Disc Transfers with Nimbie.  
Optical Media received from Audio and Moving Image Preservation is sometimes  
imaged before Digital Archives receives it. These images require file extraction.  
Occasionally, Digital Archives creates disk images of optical media.  

## Mass Optical Disc Transfers with Nimbie  
Using the Nimbie to transfer multiple discs is the standard procedure  
for discs received from CLMGT and Archival Processing.  

## Extract files from ISOs using a script  
When optical media is sent to Audio and Moving Image Preservation  
before Digital Archives the optical media might be imaged.  
When ISOs exist for optical media those images are transferred  
to Digital Archives by the Digital Repository Coordinator.  
Digital Archives then extracts files from the ISOs to facilitate  
processing.  

## Optical Media Imaging  
Digital Archives creates images of optical media when needed for emulation  
or if file transfer fails.
